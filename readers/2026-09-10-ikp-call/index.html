03ProposedOpen meeting
≈ 24 Sep

Privacy and proof-of-personhood taxonomy

Proposal: an open, topic-focused meeting in two weeks. One subject, no standing agenda, anyone may attend.

The working note already on the table separates the layers. What the taxonomy has to keep apart:

Humanity ≠ uniqueness

Two different claims, routinely collapsed into one credential.

Linkability scope

Adopt the architecture, then parameterise the dimension.

Strength expression

The headline gap — how assurance is stated, not just asserted.

Continuity

The carrier and its controls, not the primitive.

Agent authority

The field list exists; it needs completing.

Gaps nobody owns

Coercion and credential rental · threat model per category · governance of the domain.

Verifiability is not truth. A taxonomy that cannot say which of these a scheme delivers cannot be used to certify one.

Lattice Jolt

The zkVM goes post-quantum: Akita, a Module-SIS lattice commitment, replaces the curve-based Dory — the same assumption family as ML-DSA and ML-KEM.

128-bit target · >2M RISC-V cycles/s on a laptop

Project Eleven

Key recovery from state reuse

Stateful hash-based signatures carry an operational failure mode, not just a cryptographic one: sign twice from one state and the key is exposed.

Bears on XMSS / XMSS^MT and LMS / HSS
NIST SP 800-208 · RFC 8391 · RFC 8554 → S-04 / S-05 state caveat · the Poseidon2-XMSS ruling
World · Atheon · Reilabs · Nethermind
