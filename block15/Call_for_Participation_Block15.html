Call for Participation — BGIN Block 15

October 15–16, 2026 • Georgetown University, Washington, D.C., United States

Dear Past BGIN Participants,

We are excited to invite you to BGIN Block 15, which will be held at Georgetown University in Washington, D.C., United States on October 15–16, 2026. As a valued past participant in BGIN events, we would like to extend this special invitation for you to join us for this important gathering.

Block 15 will bring together stakeholders from around the world to advance blockchain governance standards and collaborate on critical projects that may become international standards through ISO/TC 307.

Register now

Why Block 15 Matters

Block 15 represents a critical milestone for BGIN, featuring:

1. Major Standardization Progress

  • Security Information Sharing Framework: Already published and submitted for ISO standardization; expected to become an ISO standard within one year.
  • Agentic AI Security Information Sharing System: Continued development, jointly with MITRE, SEAL-ISAC, and JP Crypto-ISAC.

2. Important Government & Institutional Engagement

  • Blockchain Technology Competition (PQC): Updates on the multi-year technology competition on PQC migration and key management for blockchain (similar to NIST's cryptographic competition).

Program Highlights

Up to 16 sessions across two days, with parallel sessions in two rooms. Key topics include:

Cyber Security Working Group

  • Security AI Agent Project, Offline Key Management, ST/PP, Governance of the security supply chain

Identity, Key Management & Privacy (IKP)

  • PQC Migration and Crypto Agility, Privacy Enhanced Authentication, Accountable Wallet, Forensics and Analysis, Proof of Personhood

Financial Applications & Social Economics (FASE)

  • Harmonization (crypto-asset, stablecoin, tokenized deposit), Technical Metrics for Decentralization, Practical Stablecoin Guide, Illicit Activities and Common Lexicon

Agentic AI Working Group

  • AI + Blockchain integration, Agent Hack (hands-on development sessions)

General Sessions: Opening Plenary, Blockchain Technology Competition update, Audit & securities regulation, Networking Reception
