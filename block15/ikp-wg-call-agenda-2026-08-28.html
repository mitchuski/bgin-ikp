Privacy & personhood — taxonomy updates

Three efforts are converging on proof of personhood from different layers: the World Foundation's Proof of Human Conceptual Standards Framework (v0.13, ten assurance categories), the ToIP DTG workstreams (Credentials WD01 + ZKP requirements v0.3), and the IKP proof-of-personhood paper heading to Block 15.

Tabled · “Proof of Human: Overlap and Convergence Analysis” (working note, Aug 2026)

Prepared for this WG and World Foundation; circulated alongside this agenda. It maps all ten WF assurance categories against the published DTG material and argues the WF framework lands strongest as a cross-ecosystem assurance-semantics layer — populating assurance-class / policy / accreditation fields the proof layer already discloses, with the NIST xAL / ISO 29115 mapping nobody else will write — rather than a standalone fourth vocabulary.

  • Gaps nobody owns = candidate IKP work items: coercion / duress / credential rental, an adversary-and-horizon column per assurance category, who accredits a VTN, the AML–sanctions vs. unlinkable-uniqueness collision, and the global-registry harms case.
  • Sequencing: convergence gets introduced at the BGIN-co-organised DTG session at GDC26 (day 2, 16:00–16:50); the assurance decomposition then rides into the proof-of-personhood paper as a contributed section before Block 15.

ZKP in DTG — first proof proposal, open for comments

The Digital Trust Graph ZKP work now has two concrete artifacts on the table — a running proof proposal and a new requirements record — and we are looking for comments from IKP members.

  • Proposed proof: uniqueness / nullifier with set membership and transcript binding — a scoped reuse-detection predicate (not "one unique human"), benchmarked as a working circuit with published numbers.
  • Evidence and reproduction live in a public repo with a verification registry — independent runs have already reproduced the build byte-identically across architectures: github.com/mitchuski/dtgwg-zkp-mage.
To evaluate · ADR-001 “Community-Anchored Proof” (Proposed, 25 Aug 2026)

docs.fpp.storm.ws/dtg-community-anchored-proof-adr.html — proposes the first ZK use case against DTG credentials: prove in one presentation that (1) the holder has a valid relationship credential, (2) the holder has valid membership in community C, and (3) the relationship's issuer also holds valid membership in C — without revealing relationship, counterparty, or persistent identifiers. Requirements-only and deliberately format-agnostic: 26 testable clauses across privacy, soundness, currency, registry, deployment, conformance, and governance.

Initial evaluation, for discussion:

  • Strongly convergent with the proof proposal above — clause 3 is the "verifiable edge" question already under discussion in the credentials-spec threads, and the membership leg, challenge/transcript binding (S5), and published-test-vector + cross-implementation pattern (X1/X2) already exist as runnable evidence, with circuit benchmarks that can seed the D1/D2 device and verifier budgets the ADR leaves open.
  • The hard seam is the registry (T1–T4, C1–C4): membership and revocation state exposed in proof-compatible form, with currency checks that do not identify the holder — precisely the registry–ZK interaction the ZKP work holds as deferred charter scope. This is where cross-WG coordination is actually needed.
  • One clarification to raise: P4 full unlinkability vs. reuse detection — if communities want Sybil resistance the construction needs scoped nullifiers, which are governed, declared linkability rather than a leak; the ADR should say which contexts require them.

Ask: members review the clauses before or shortly after the call, and we agree where comments land (the ADR's own channel vs. the ZKP task-force threads).
